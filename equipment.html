Equipment recommendations

The right item, clearly explained and approved by you.

VIPOAP is not a general online shop. An Engineer Partner may recommend a supported item when it genuinely helps solve your problem.

No equipment markup at launch.

You see the exact item and price. Equipment is a separate transaction and is paid in full before it is purchased.

1

Recommendation

Your Engineer Partner selects an approved item and explains its purpose, price, setup requirements and known issues.

2

Your approval

You approve the exact item and pay its cost in advance. These funds are kept separate from support earnings.

3

Purchase and receipt

The exact item is ordered and a purchase receipt is uploaded. Any lower price is refunded or credited; a higher price requires fresh approval.

Supported categories

  • Mesh Wi-Fi and selected routers
  • Cables, adapters and computer accessories
  • Printers and scanning equipment
  • Selected smart-home equipment

Returns and changes

Returns, refunds and price differences are tracked against the equipment transaction. Engineer Partners may not privately sell equipment through a VIPOAP job.
