How a secure session works

  1. You book remote support or contact VIPOAP using a trusted number.
  2. Your Engineer Partner explains whether phone, video or remote computer support is appropriate.
  3. You approve any remote connection yourself and can end it at any time.
  4. The session ends when the work is complete; no permanent access remains.

We will never ask for

  • Your full password, PIN or security answer
  • Your card details through a remote-access window
  • Remote access during an unexpected call
  • Permanent unattended access to your device
