# Website Requirements V2 implementation

This file tracks the implementation brief supplied in August 2026. It is an operational checklist, not legal advice.

## Implemented in the public website and booking journey

- Inclusive positioning for all ages, while retaining patient support for older and less-confident customers.
- Equal promotion of home visits and remote support.
- Home visit pricing: £30 first hour and £10 per agreed additional 30 minutes.
- Remote pricing: £15 first 30 minutes, £25 up to 60 minutes and £10 per agreed additional 30 minutes.
- £15 late-cancellation/no-show wording, free cancellation until one hour before the appointment and exceptional-circumstance waiver wording.
- Payment-after-support wording and separate prepaid equipment-purchase wording.
- Booking fields for support type, address, postcode, problem description, preferred time, contact details, notification channel and optional accessibility notes.
- “Book for myself” and “Book for someone else” journeys, including the authorised family contact, relationship and consent reminder.
- Booking and engineer-facing warnings prohibiting storage of passwords, PINs, card details, recovery codes and security answers.
- Dedicated Remote Support, Safety & Trust and Equipment pages.
- Remote-access warnings: no unexpected access calls and no permanent unattended access.
- Booking records now preserve support type, address, notification preference and accessibility notes.
- Cash/card/online payment method support in call-out records; paid cash records receive an electronic timestamp.
- Cancellation charge, waiver and waiver-reason support in booking records.
- Engineer Partner recruitment pricing and no-guaranteed-work wording.
- Existing platform coverage: customers, equipment inventories and files; call-outs, notes, costs, follow-ups and photos; territories, operators, roles, DBS uploads, training, exports, invoices and franchise reporting.

## Next system phases

1. Engineer matching by geocoded five-mile radius and availability, with staged disclosure of personal details.
2. Online/card payment provider, automated multi-channel receipts and refunds.
3. Weekly Engineer Partner settlement ledger and cash-offset reconciliation.
4. Full Engineer Partner application-status workflow and £99 onboarding payment.
5. Supported-equipment catalogue, customer proposal/approval, ring-fenced equipment funds, receipt and return tracking.
6. Safeguarding/incident case management, unsafe-appointment flagging and suspension workflow.
7. Complaints, coaching/warning/suspension, seven-day rework cases and refund/credit/return-visit outcomes.
8. Access logging and deeper least-privilege controls around accepted jobs.

## Mandatory launch gate

Before contractual wording or live marketplace payments are enabled, obtain professional review of employment status, agency/VAT structure, equipment-fund accounting, consumer rights, DBS eligibility, insurance, UK GDPR roles, safeguarding, the Engineer Agreement and Customer Terms.
