# Franchisee onboarding checklist

No territory goes live until every mandatory item has an owner, evidence and approval date.

## Due diligence and agreement

- [ ] Candidate values and customer-care interview completed.
- [ ] Identity, right-to-work and company details verified as applicable.
- [ ] References and financial suitability review completed.
- [ ] Role-specific criminal-record-check eligibility assessed; checks completed where lawful and appropriate.
- [ ] Franchise agreement independently reviewed, signed and cooling-off/review process completed.
- [ ] Territory postcode sectors and boundary map agreed.
- [ ] Fees, reporting dates and payment method agreed.

## Insurance, safety and compliance

- [ ] Required public liability, professional indemnity, motor/business use, employer and cyber cover evidenced.
- [ ] Data-protection responsibilities and privacy training completed.
- [ ] Lone-working, home-visit, safeguarding and incident procedures acknowledged.
- [ ] Secure device, screen lock, updates, encryption and recovery contacts confirmed.
- [ ] Photo/file consent and secure disposal procedures understood.

## Brand and operations

- [ ] Brand standards and approved artwork supplied.
- [ ] Vehicle, clothing, print and digital materials approved before production.
- [ ] Core services, call-out inclusions, exclusions, memberships and pricing assessment passed.
- [ ] Enquiry, booking, visit, invoicing, aftercare and complaint procedures assessed.
- [ ] Customer communication and plain-language standards demonstrated.

## VIPOAP OS

- [ ] Named operator account created with only the assigned territory.
- [ ] Two-step email access tested; no shared login used.
- [ ] Test customer, booking, call-out, equipment record, file, invoice and follow-up completed.
- [ ] Territory report and export reconciled against the test records.
- [ ] Lost-device, leaver and suspected-data-incident process tested.

## Practical sign-off

- [ ] Knowledge assessment passed.
- [ ] Observed visits with the founding operation completed.
- [ ] Supervised visits in the new territory completed.
- [ ] Vehicle/equipment/toolkit inspection passed.
- [ ] First-week diary, local launch plan and escalation contacts confirmed.
- [ ] Franchisor issues written go-live approval.

## First 90 days

- [ ] Daily record-completeness review during week one.
- [ ] Weekly coaching and performance review during month one.
- [ ] Fortnightly review during months two and three.
- [ ] Sample customer follow-up calls completed by the franchisor.
- [ ] 30-, 60- and 90-day gates passed or corrective plan agreed.
