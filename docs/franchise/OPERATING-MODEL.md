# VIPOAP franchise operating model

## Purpose

VIPOAP provides patient, friendly home technology support that removes worry for customers and their families. Expansion must increase local availability without weakening trust, safety, pricing clarity or service quality.

Andover is the founding territory and operating benchmark. The first external territory should be treated as a pilot, not proof that the model is ready for rapid national sale.

## Roles

### Franchisor

VIPOAP centrally owns and controls the brand, service standards, training, approved systems, website, customer promises, national marketing framework, reporting definitions and operating manual. It audits performance, supports operators and can require corrective action.

### Franchisee

The franchisee operates the agreed territory, supplies suitable people and vehicles, follows approved pricing and procedures, protects customer data, maintains required insurance and records every customer interaction in VIPOAP OS.

### Operator

An operator may only access assigned territories. They must use their own account, record visits promptly, obtain customer approval before extra work or cost, and escalate safeguarding, security, complaint or data concerns.

## Territory principles

- Define territories using named postcode sectors plus a clear map and customer-allocation rule.
- Confirm whether exclusivity is conditional on minimum service and performance standards.
- Allocate online enquiries using the service address, not the customer's billing address.
- Record boundary exceptions and centrally managed accounts in VIPOAP OS.
- Do not sell overlapping rights or promise national-account income without written rules.

## Commercial controls

The franchise agreement should define the initial fee, royalty basis, brand or marketing contribution, software/support charges, payment dates and audit rights. The admin system already holds royalty and brand-fund percentages per territory; the legal agreement remains the authority.

Core pricing, call-out inclusions, cancellation terms, membership benefits and national promotions are centrally controlled. Locally varied pricing must be approved and clearly shown before booking.

## Standard customer journey

1. Enquiry is captured against one customer record.
2. Eligibility, location, accessibility and contact preferences are confirmed.
3. Scope, call-out price and likely exclusions are explained before booking.
4. Booking is assigned to the correct territory and operator.
5. Operator identifies themselves, confirms consent and completes the agreed work.
6. Equipment, actions, time, notes, files, recommendations and costs are logged.
7. Invoice or membership entitlement is recorded; no unexpected work is charged.
8. Customer receives a completion summary and security advice where relevant.
9. Follow-up is scheduled and the outcome recorded.
10. Complaints, risk concerns and possible upgrades follow the defined escalation route.

## Data and systems

VIPOAP OS is the required system of record for customers, bookings, call-outs, equipment, documents, memberships, invoices and follow-ups. Personal accounts must not be shared. Access is limited by role and territory, important changes are audited, and access must be removed immediately when a person leaves.

Only necessary information should be recorded. Sensitive notes must be factual and relevant. Customer information must not be copied into personal notebooks, messaging accounts or unapproved cloud storage. A retention schedule, incident procedure, privacy responsibilities and controller/processor arrangements must be legally reviewed before the pilot.

## Safety and customer care

- Never request or retain a customer's passwords unless an approved secure procedure specifically requires it.
- Never initiate banking transactions or make financial decisions for a customer.
- Explain changes in plain language and obtain consent before proceeding.
- Record pre-existing damage and use photographs only with permission.
- Stop and escalate suspected fraud, abuse, coercion, immediate danger or an unsafe working environment.
- Do not describe DBS checks as universally required; obtain role-specific eligibility advice.
- Provide reasonable adjustments and allow a trusted family member or advocate to participate with the customer's consent.

## Quality controls

Each territory is reviewed monthly against: response time, booking conversion, arrival reliability, first-time resolution, average invoice, membership conversion, follow-up completion, repeat visits, complaints, refunds, customer satisfaction and record completeness.

Red flags include unlogged work, unexplained discounts, missing costs, shared accounts, work outside territory, unresolved complaints, skipped follow-ups or repeated brand breaches. Serious customer-safety, fraud or data issues are escalated immediately.

## Minimum franchise-ready pack still requiring professional completion

- Franchise agreement and disclosure/recruitment process.
- Financial model with evidence-based earnings assumptions and sensitivity testing.
- Trademark and intellectual-property review.
- Insurance schedule and approved policy minimums.
- Data protection roles, retention schedule and incident response.
- Health and safety, lone-working and safeguarding policies.
- Recruitment, employment-status and subcontractor documentation.
- Full training curriculum, assessments and annual recertification.
- Complaints, refunds, business continuity and franchise exit procedures.
