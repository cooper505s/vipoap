# VIPOAP brand standards

## Approved identity

- Name: **VIPOAP** (no space, except where a platform limitation requires one).
- Primary font: **Quicksand** for headings, body copy, controls and branded materials.
- Navy: `#102957`.
- Green: `#5A8F5A`.
- Light green: `#7DBE7D`.
- Cream: `#F7F3EC`.
- Descriptor beneath the VIPOAP name: **Technology without the worry.**
- Do not add a second, repetitive tagline beneath the header logo.

The approved heart/Wi-Fi mark uses consistent solid green for the darker bars and light green for the two top bars. Use approved master artwork; do not redraw, recolour, stretch, crop tightly, add effects or place a white-backed version on cream.

## Voice

Write as a calm, patient person would speak. Use short sentences, plain English and specific reassurance. Avoid fear, age stereotypes, pressure selling, unexplained technical language and claims that cannot be evidenced.

## Customer promises

- Friendly, patient support.
- Clear prices before work begins.
- No unexpected extras.
- Respect for the customer's home, choices and privacy.
- A clear summary of what changed.
- Aftercare and agreed follow-up recorded in VIPOAP OS.

## Local use

Franchisees may add the approved local territory name and contact details in designated templates. They may not create alternative logos, fonts, taglines, colours, service promises, social profiles or promotional prices without approval. Vehicle graphics, clothing, leaflets, paid adverts and new digital layouts require central approval before publication or production.

The approved settings in the Franchise section of VIPOAP OS are the operational source of truth. Brand changes must be owner-approved and are recorded in the audit log.
