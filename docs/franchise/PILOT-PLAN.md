# First franchise territory pilot plan

## Objective

Prove that a trained operator outside Andover can deliver the same safe, friendly and commercially sustainable VIPOAP service using the same systems and controls.

## Entry gates

Do not start recruitment until Andover has at least three months of reliable baseline data for demand, travel time, job duration, revenue, repeat visits, memberships, aftercare and complaints. Do not go live until the legal, financial, insurance and data-protection documents listed in the operating model have professional approval.

## Territory selection

Choose one territory close enough for practical mentoring but separate enough to test allocation and local marketing. Prefer a compact drive-time area with a suitable older population, reliable operator availability and no ambiguous postcode overlap. Begin with a controlled postcode list rather than a whole county.

## 90-day sequence

### Preparation: weeks -6 to 0

- Validate postcode boundaries, travel assumptions and local demand.
- Complete agreement, onboarding, training and supervised visits.
- Configure territory, operator access, approved pricing and reporting.
- Run end-to-end test enquiries and a data/access incident exercise.
- Approve local web, vehicle, clothing and print materials.

### Supported launch: days 1–30

- Limit diary capacity and service radius.
- Review every completed record and first ten invoices.
- Hold weekly coaching; sample customer aftercare independently.
- Franchisor remains available for live escalation and complex jobs.

### Controlled growth: days 31–60

- Increase capacity only if record quality and customer care remain on target.
- Test membership presentation, local partnerships and repeat/referral demand.
- Compare actual drive time, job duration and acquisition cost with the model.

### Validation: days 61–90

- Review customer, operational, financial and compliance results.
- Document deviations and update training or systems.
- Decide: approve normal operation, extend the pilot with conditions, pause, or exit.

## Pilot scorecard

Targets must be set from the Andover baseline before launch. At minimum measure:

- enquiries, bookings and conversion rate;
- arrival reliability and response time;
- first-time resolution and repeat visit rate;
- average job time, travel time and invoice value;
- membership offers, take-up and retention;
- follow-ups completed on time;
- customer rating, compliments, complaints and refunds;
- complete customer/call-out/equipment/cost records;
- gross margin and cash collection;
- brand, security, safety and territory exceptions.

Any serious customer-safety, fraud or data breach triggers immediate review regardless of commercial performance.

## Scale decision

Do not recruit territory two merely because territory one has sold. Scale only after the pilot has completed 90 days, the unit economics reconcile to real records, support workload is understood, corrective actions are closed, and the operating manual has been updated from evidence.
