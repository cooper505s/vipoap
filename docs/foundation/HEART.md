# Heart

Heart is the visual expression of reassurance within VIPOAP.

Heart is not a talking cartoon character. It does not replace Dan, pretend to be human or draw attention away from the task. It reacts quietly to what is happening and helps the interface feel calm and supportive.

## Role

Heart may be used to:

- welcome a member;
- show that something is being checked;
- confirm a booking or successful action;
- reinforce a safety warning;
- reassure after an error;
- mark a completed visit or resolved request.

## Motion principles

All motion must be:

- subtle;
- slow enough to feel calm;
- brief;
- optional for people who prefer reduced motion;
- free from flashing or rapid repetition.

## Approved states

### Calm

A very gentle single pulse, used sparingly.

### Thinking

A soft glow or slow breathing effect while a short task is running.

### Success

One small pulse followed by a settled state.

### Protected

A simple shield or tick appears around Heart when a scam warning or safety action has been completed.

### Needs attention

Heart may be paired with amber or red status styling, but it must not shake, flash or create panic.

### Error

Heart remains calm. The accompanying words explain what happened and what to do next.

## Usage rules

- Never animate continuously without a clear reason.
- Never use movement purely as decoration.
- Never distort the approved logo geometry.
- Never make Heart childish, noisy or comedic.
- Never use sound by default.
- Respect the operating system's reduced-motion preference.

## Example messages

Booking confirmed:

> You are all booked in.

Scam warning:

> I would be careful with this one.

Error:

> Something did not work. Your information is still here, so you can try again.
