# Calm Design

## Purpose
Calm Design is the visual and interaction philosophy for every VIPOAP experience. It should lower anxiety, make choices obvious and help people feel supported.

## Core principles

1. **One clear task at a time.** Each screen should have one obvious purpose.
2. **Large, readable text.** Important information must not depend on small type.
3. **Comfortable spacing.** Content should breathe and never feel crowded.
4. **Plain English.** Prefer familiar words over technical language.
5. **Gentle motion.** Movement should reassure, never distract or rush.
6. **Consistent patterns.** Buttons, forms and messages should behave the same everywhere.
7. **Visible reassurance.** Explain what will happen before an action and what happened afterwards.
8. **Easy recovery.** Mistakes should be simple to correct without losing work.

## Approved palette

- Cream: `#F7F3EC`
- Green: `#5A8F5A`
- Light green: `#7DBE7D`
- Navy: `#102957`

Additional colours may only be introduced for defined status meanings and must meet accessibility requirements.

## Layout

- Use a comfortable maximum reading width.
- Prefer single-column forms.
- Keep primary actions near the related content.
- Avoid dense dashboards and long unbroken paragraphs.
- Use cards only when they improve grouping. Never place a decorative box around the approved header logo.

## Components

### Buttons

- Use direct labels such as **Book a visit**, **Check this message** or **Call Dan**.
- Make primary buttons easy to see and tap.
- Avoid vague labels such as **Submit** or **Continue** where a clearer action is possible.

### Forms

- Put labels above fields.
- Explain why sensitive information is requested.
- Preserve entered information after a validation error.
- Show errors beside the relevant field in calm, helpful language.

### Cards

- Use rounded corners consistently.
- Use subtle separation rather than heavy shadows.
- Do not put every section inside a card.

## Motion

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No flashing, rapid movement or endless decorative animation.
- Heart animations should be brief, subtle and meaningful.

## Review tests

Every design must pass:

- The Kitchen Table Test
- The Margaret Test
- The Calm Test
- The one-handed mobile test
- Keyboard and screen-reader checks
