# VIPOAP Vision

## Technology without the worry

VIPOAP exists to help people feel confident with technology.

Technology is now part of everyday life. It keeps families connected, supports independent living, provides access to services and helps people manage their homes. But it can also create stress, confusion and fear—especially when support is rushed, technical or impersonal.

VIPOAP is here to change that.

## Our purpose

To remove worry from everyday technology.

## Our mission

To provide friendly, patient and trustworthy home technology support in plain English, helping people stay connected, stay safe and stay independent.

## Our vision

To become the UK's most trusted home technology companion—starting in Andover and growing one home at a time.

## Our promise

We will:

- put people before technology;
- explain clearly and patiently;
- never make anyone feel embarrassed for asking;
- recommend what is right for the person, not what makes us the most money;
- protect privacy and personal information;
- help families stay connected and safe;
- leave every home better than we found it.

## Our guiding thought

> You're never on your own with technology.

## What success looks like

VIPOAP succeeds when someone finishes an interaction feeling calmer, safer and more confident than when they started.
