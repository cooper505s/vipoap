# VIPOAP Operating Procedures

## Purpose

These procedures define the minimum routines needed to operate VIPOAP reliably while the business is small. They are intentionally simple and can be expanded as customer volume grows.

## Daily

- Review new bookings, help requests and scam-check escalations.
- Confirm urgent or time-sensitive requests.
- Check today’s visits and customer contact details.
- Record meaningful outcomes after each visit or support interaction.
- Escalate any suspected safeguarding, fraud or data incident.

## Weekly

- Review open requests and identify anything waiting too long.
- Check the next four weeks of availability.
- Block unavailable dates and add any exceptional opening hours.
- Review failed emails, form errors and application alerts.
- Confirm that important repository changes have been merged and deployed as expected.
- Back up or export key records where the current platform procedure requires it.

## Monthly

- Review common support themes and update guides where useful.
- Review scam-check results for unsafe reassurance or confusing wording.
- Check account usage against free-tier limits.
- Review administrator and third-party access.
- Test one booking, one support request and one admin change end to end.
- Review privacy, deletion and retention tasks due that month.

## Booking handling

1. A booking request is received.
2. Availability is validated before acceptance.
3. The member receives a clear acknowledgement.
4. Dan confirms or rearranges the visit where necessary.
5. The visit outcome is recorded.
6. Relevant follow-up and recommendations are sent.

## Ask for Help handling

- Use friendly subjects that describe the problem rather than internal jargon.
- Keep the member informed when waiting for information or a visit.
- Record internal notes separately from customer-visible replies.
- Close a request only when the outcome and any next step are clear.

## Scam escalation handling

If money, credentials, remote access or threats are involved:

- Advise the member to stop contact with the suspected scammer.
- Direct them to independently contact the bank or relevant organisation.
- Do not ask the member to send passwords, PINs or full security codes.
- Record only information needed to support the case.
- Encourage reporting through appropriate official channels where relevant.

## Data requests

Requests to access, correct or delete personal data must be logged, identity-checked proportionately and completed within the applicable legal timescale. Do not delete records that must be retained for a lawful reason without documenting that reason.

## Incidents

An incident includes suspected data exposure, unauthorised access, lost administrative access, dangerous AI advice, misdirected customer email or a booking failure that could materially affect a member.

For every incident:

1. Contain the immediate problem.
2. Preserve relevant evidence.
3. Assess who may be affected.
4. Correct or disable the faulty service.
5. Communicate honestly where required.
6. Record the cause and prevention action.

## Manual fallback

During an outage, members must still be able to call 07977 254158 or email help@vipoap.co.uk. Bookings and requests may be recorded manually and entered into VIPOAP Core once service is restored.

## Change control

Changes affecting customer data, booking rules, email delivery, authentication or AI behaviour require a pull request, a test record and a clear deployment note.
