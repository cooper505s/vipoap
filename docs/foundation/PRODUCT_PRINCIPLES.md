# Product Principles

Every VIPOAP feature must help people feel more confident, safe or supported with technology.

## Principles

1. **People before technology**  
   Start with the person's worry, goal or situation—not the device or system.

2. **One household, one story**  
   Bookings, visits, devices, scam checks and help requests should contribute to one coherent household history.

3. **Confidence over dependency**  
   Help people understand and do more for themselves where appropriate.

4. **Human help remains visible**  
   Automation and AI must never make Dan difficult to reach.

5. **Plain-English outcomes**  
   Customer-facing language should describe what is happening, not expose internal system terminology.

6. **Safe by default**  
   Protect privacy, minimise stored data, use least privilege and avoid high-risk actions without confirmation.

7. **Accessible from the start**  
   Accessibility is part of design, development and testing—not a final audit.

8. **Small useful releases**  
   Deliver the simplest version that solves a real problem, observe its use and improve it.

9. **Free-tier first, not fragile**  
   Prefer sustainable free services during early growth, while keeping clear upgrade paths and avoiding unnecessary lock-in.

10. **Every interaction adds value**  
    Visits and support should improve the household record, future advice and the member's confidence.

## Feature approval questions

Before a feature enters development, answer:

- Who is this helping?
- What worry or difficulty does it reduce?
- Can it be explained in one sentence?
- Could Margaret use it without assistance?
- What personal data does it need, and why?
- What happens when it fails?
- Is human support still easy to reach?
- How will we know it has helped?

## Release priorities

The initial public release prioritises:

1. A clear and reassuring public website.
2. Easy contact and booking.
3. An installable PWA.
4. A simple scam-checking journey.
5. A lightweight help-request system.
6. A usable admin view for managing availability and enquiries.

Customer accounts, device records, memberships and advanced AI follow only after the public experience is reliable and tested with real users.
