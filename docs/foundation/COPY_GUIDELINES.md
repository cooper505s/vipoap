# Copy Guidelines

## Voice

VIPOAP sounds calm, friendly, patient, honest and capable. We never sound technical for the sake of it, overfamiliar, patronising or alarmist.

## Writing rules

- Address the reader as **you**.
- Use **I** when Dan is speaking personally and **we** when describing VIPOAP as a service.
- Prefer short sentences and common words.
- Lead with what the person needs to know or do.
- Use active voice.
- Explain what happens next.
- Never make someone feel foolish for asking.
- Avoid urgency unless immediate action is genuinely required for safety.

## Preferred language

Use:

- Ask for help
- Book a visit
- Check if it is a scam
- My Home
- Visit summary
- Home Health Check
- Trusted family contact

Avoid customer-facing terms such as:

- ticket
- incident
- endpoint
- authentication failure
- asset register
- SLA
- remediation
- AI analysis

## Calls to action

Labels should describe the outcome:

- **Call Dan**
- **Book a home visit**
- **Check this message**
- **Send this to Dan**
- **Save my availability**

Avoid generic labels such as **Submit**, **Proceed** or **Click here**.

## Error messages

Use this structure:

1. Say what happened.
2. Reassure the person that their work is safe where possible.
3. Explain the next step.

Example:

> We could not send your request just now. Your details are still here, so please wait a moment and try again. You can also call Dan on 07977 254158.

## Scam checker language

Never guarantee that a message is safe or fraudulent. Use clear risk language:

- **This looks likely to be a scam.**
- **I would be careful with this.**
- **There is not enough information to be sure.**
- **This may be genuine, but check it independently before acting.**

Always provide safe next steps and encourage the person not to click links, share security information or send money while uncertain.

## Reading check

Before publishing, ask:

- Could this be understood on the first reading?
- Is any technical term unnecessary?
- Does the reader know what happens next?
- Does the wording feel calm and respectful?
