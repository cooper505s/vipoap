# VIPOAP Brand Guidelines

## Brand name

Use **VIPOAP** in uppercase. Do not expand it as an acronym in customer-facing material.

Preferred strapline:

> Technology without the worry.

Supporting line:

> Home Technology Support

## Logo

The approved heart-and-Wi-Fi logo is locked artwork.

Do not:

- redraw the heart or signal bars;
- change the proportions;
- substitute fonts in the wordmark;
- stretch or squash the logo;
- place it inside an added card, box or border;
- recolour it outside the approved palette;
- regenerate it using AI.

## Core palette

- Light green: `#7DBE7D`
- Green: `#5A8F5A`
- Navy: `#102957`
- Cream: `#F7F3EC`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Colour meaning

- Green: reassurance, progress and confirmation.
- Amber: caution or something needing attention.
- Red: urgent action or immediate risk.
- Navy: trust, clarity and important information.
- Cream: warmth and calm.

## Typography

Typography must be highly readable, friendly and consistent.

Primary typeface: **Quicksand**

- Regular body copy: `400`
- Supporting emphasis: `500`
- Navigation and labels: `600`
- Headings, buttons and supporting display text: `700`

The approved VIPOAP name must remain part of the locked logo artwork. The website must self-host Quicksand for all live text. Use `"Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif` only as technical fallbacks while the font loads.

Rules:

- use one primary font family across the website, app and portal;
- keep body text comfortably large;
- avoid decorative scripts and condensed fonts;
- use strong contrast and generous line spacing;
- never rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.

## Tone of voice

VIPOAP sounds:

- calm;
- patient;
- warm;
- professional;
- honest;
- reassuring.

VIPOAP never sounds:

- patronising;
- sarcastic;
- rushed;
- overly technical;
- alarmist;
- sales-driven.

## Writing examples

Do not write:

> Authentication failed.

Write:

> That did not work. Let us try again.

Do not write:

> Invalid email address.

Write:

> That email address does not look quite right.

Do not write:

> Submit a support ticket.

Write:

> Ask for help.
