Our local promise

Customers are supported by someone from their area

VIPOAP territories are run by local people who understand their community and specialise in friendly home technology support. Customers get a consistent national standard with a familiar local contact.

If we are not operating in an area yet, we are sorry that we cannot currently offer a local visit. We are expanding carefully, with the aim of eventually covering the whole of the UK.

What the territory receives

  • An agreed local postcode territory
  • A local operator profile and contact route
  • Suitable website and booking leads allocated by service address
  • VIPOAP OS for customers, visits, equipment, follow-ups and reporting
  • Brand, technical, safety and customer-care training
  • Operating standards, templates and ongoing support

Lead volume and income are not guaranteed. They depend on local demand, availability, marketing, service quality and territory maturity.

How joining works

From initial conversation to supported local launch

We assess technical ability and customer care equally. Being brilliant with technology is not enough without patience, trust and reliable record keeping.

1

Register

Tell us about your area, experience, availability and why VIPOAP interests you. We normally aim to review an enquiry within five working days.

2

Discovery

We discuss territory availability, local demand, commercial expectations and whether the model suits both sides. Allow one to two weeks.

3

Checks and training

Subject to fit, we complete due diligence, agreements, insurance, appropriate checks and practical training. This will commonly take four to eight weeks.

4

Supported launch

Your initial diary is controlled while records and customer outcomes are reviewed. A typical route from first discussion to launch may take eight to twelve weeks.

Timings are indicative, not promises. Territory checks, legal documentation, training readiness and individual circumstances may make the process shorter or longer.

What we look for

Technical confidence with a patient service mindset

Technical capability

  • Confident troubleshooting Wi-Fi, routers and mesh networks
  • Comfortable with phones, tablets, Windows computers, printers and smart TVs
  • Good awareness of accounts, backups, updates, passwords and scams
  • A methodical approach and the judgement to stop or escalate unfamiliar work
  • Willingness to keep learning and complete practical assessments

Professional readiness

  • Patient, calm communication and respect for older or vulnerable customers
  • Reliable local transport and ability to attend home visits
  • Secure devices, accurate records and comfort using a business operating system
  • Suitable insurance and willingness to complete lawful background checks where appropriate
  • Commitment to VIPOAP pricing, brand, privacy, safeguarding and aftercare standards
