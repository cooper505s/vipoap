How VIPOAP keeps you safe

Trust is part of the service.

Clear identities, careful privacy rules and calm explanations help you stay in control from booking through to aftercare.

Never share passwords or banking security details.

Our notes must not contain passwords, PINs, card details, recovery codes or security answers.

Checked Engineer Partners

Partners complete identity and address checks, relevant vetting, training, assessment and approval before activation. DBS information is described according to the check actually completed; we do not claim every person holds an Enhanced DBS.

Recognisable visits

Your appointment is confirmed in advance. Engineer Partners carry branded photo identification, and customer details are released only when needed for an accepted appointment.

Safe remote support

Remote access is approved by you for a specific booked session. We never unexpectedly call asking for access and never leave permanent unattended access.

Safeguarding and complaints

Partners receive safeguarding and scam-awareness training. Concerns are escalated centrally, and serious cases can prevent a Partner receiving new jobs while they are investigated.

Seven-day workmanship support

If the same issue returns within seven days, contact us. We will review the original work and agree an appropriate rework, refund, credit or return visit.

Clear contact route

Call 07977 254158 or email help@vipoap.co.uk. If you feel in immediate danger, end the visit or call and contact the emergency services.
