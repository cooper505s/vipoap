Common questions

Before joining

Can a family member arrange the plan?

Yes. A relative can contact VIPOAP and help organise support for someone they care about.

Are repairs and new equipment included?

The plans cover the support listed above. Parts, equipment and additional work are quoted separately before anything is agreed.

When do Complete visits become available?

One included one-hour visit becomes available after each six months of active membership. Visits do not carry into a later membership year.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There is no long contract and you can cancel monthly. Future unused benefits end when membership ends.
