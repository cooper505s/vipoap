Common questions

Before joining

Do I need a membership to use VIPOAP?

No. Pay As You Go has no subscription or joining fee, and you can simply book help whenever you need it.

Do unused remote-support minutes roll over?

No. The 30-minute allowance resets at the beginning of each membership billing period and does not accumulate.

How does the Family allowance work?

The Family plan includes 30 remote-support minutes for the membership as a whole. For example, if one family member uses 20 minutes, 10 included minutes remain that month.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancelling stops future renewals, and your benefits continue until the end of the period already paid for. Annual plans remain active until the end of their paid annual period.

Are repairs and equipment included?

No. Equipment and any work outside the listed benefits are explained and priced separately before you agree.
