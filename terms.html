Simple terms for using this website

These terms explain how the VIPOAP website and enquiry services may be used.

Information on this website

The website provides general information about VIPOAP services. Guides are intended as helpful general advice and are not a substitute for professional financial, legal, medical or emergency support.

Bookings and enquiries

Submitting a form or requesting a time does not create a confirmed appointment until VIPOAP confirms it. Any price, scope and equipment cost will be explained before paid work begins.

Scam and safety guidance

Scam guidance is provided as a second opinion and cannot guarantee that a message, caller or website is genuine. Do not send money, share passwords or reveal security codes when unsure. Contact your bank or the police promptly where appropriate.

Availability and changes

Website content, opening times and services may be updated. VIPOAP may pause or withdraw parts of the website for maintenance or security reasons.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the website, attempt unauthorised access, submit harmful material or interfere with other visitors' use of the service.
