Meet Dan

Helping people feel confident, not dependent

I have worked in IT for more than 30 years, but the part I have always enjoyed most is helping people understand and enjoy their technology.

VIPOAP grew from helping family, friends and neighbours with Wi-Fi, phones, tablets, printers, televisions and online safety. I realised many people did not need a repair shop or a call centre. They needed someone patient, local and trustworthy to sit beside them and explain things properly.

That is exactly what every VIPOAP visit is designed to provide.
