Support for someone you care about

Trusted local technology help for your parents

If you live too far away to help in person, or technology is not your area, VIPOAP can be the patient local contact your parent can rely on.

You stay involved

  • Arrange and pay for support
  • Nominate yourself as the family contact
  • Request visit updates with your parent's consent
  • Keep equipment and visit history in one place
  • Manage ongoing support from a distance
How family-managed support works

Reassurance for you, confidence for your parent

1

Tell us about your parent

Share their area, what they need help with and how they prefer to communicate.

2

Agree permissions

Your parent remains in control. We record who may arrange visits, receive updates and manage payment.

3

Meet the local specialist

A trained VIPOAP operator visits patiently, records the work completed and explains what happens next.

4

Manage ongoing help

You can arrange future visits or a support plan, while your parent has a familiar local person to call.

What can be included

A practical support circle around your parent

  • Wi-Fi, phones, tablets, computers, printers and smart televisions
  • Help checking suspicious calls, messages and emails
  • Equipment records and recommendations
  • Visit notes, costs, agreed follow-ups and aftercare
  • Priority booking and regular check-ins through a support plan

Privacy comes first

We only share visit information with an authorised family contact when the customer has agreed. Sensitive passwords, banking details and private communications are never included in routine updates.

For urgent safety, medical or safeguarding concerns, contact the appropriate emergency or specialist service.
