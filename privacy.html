Privacy

Your details are treated with care

This page explains what information VIPOAP collects and how it is used.

In plain English

Your details are used only to answer your enquiry, arrange a visit or provide the service you asked for.

What I may collect

Your name, telephone number, email address, preferred contact time and the information you choose to include in a message.

How your details are used

They are used to respond to you, arrange appointments, provide support and keep appropriate business records.

Who receives your details

Your details are not sold. The callback form uses FormSubmit to deliver your message by email, so the details entered are processed by that service to send the enquiry.

How long details are kept

Enquiries are kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, service and any legal or accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask what information is held about you, request a correction or ask for information to be deleted where there is no legal reason to keep it.

Contact

For privacy questions, email help@vipoap.co.uk or call 07977 254158.
