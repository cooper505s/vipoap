    Mesh Wi-Fi in plain English

    Sometimes one router cannot cover the whole home

    A mesh system uses a few small Wi-Fi points around the house. They work together so your phone, tablet, printer and television can stay connected as you move between rooms.

    • Better coverage upstairs and downstairs
    • More reliable streaming and video calls
    • Fewer dead spots
    • One simple Wi-Fi name and password

    Common questions

    Before booking a Wi-Fi visit

    Do I need to buy a mesh system?

    Not always. The problem may be the router position, a device setting, the broadband service or interference. We will check before recommending equipment.

    Can you help with routers supplied by BT, Sky, Virgin Media or another provider?

    Yes. We can help check the setup, improve placement and connect your devices. Some line or account problems may still need the provider to resolve them.

    Can you help with Wi-Fi in the garden or an outside office?

    Often, yes. The right solution depends on distance, walls, power and where the internet connection enters the home.

    Will I lose my current Wi-Fi name and password?

    Usually we can keep things familiar, but We will explain any change before making it and help reconnect your devices.
